# 🌍 Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard** is a Power BI practice project developed to gain hands-on experience and strengthen my data analysis and data visualization skills.

This project helped me explore new Power BI concepts and features while analyzing Airbnb's global performance across 10 cities.

The dashboard provides insights into listing growth, city market share, property prices, customer reviews, and ratings.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Listings:** 279,712
- **Total Cities:** 10
- **Total Hosts:** 182,024
- **Property Types:** 144
- **Total Reviews:** 5,373K

## 📈 Dashboard Screenshots

### 1. New Listings — Airbnb Growth Analysis

![New Listings Dashboard](Overview.png)

This dashboard visualizes Airbnb's listing growth over time from 2008 to 2020, categorized by different property types.

**Key Insights:**

- Airbnb reached its highest number of new listings in 2015.
- Listing growth was restrained in 2016 and 2017 due to tighter local regulations.
- Airbnb became profitable in the second half of 2016.
- 2017 marked the first full year of generating income.
- Growth resumed from 2018 and was impacted by COVID-19.

### 2. Market Share by City & Ratings Analysis

![Market Share and Ratings Dashboard](Ratings.png)

This dashboard analyzes Airbnb's market share across cities, average prices by property type, and customer ratings.

**Key Insights:**

- Paris, New York, and Sydney account for almost half of total listings and 59% of total reviews.
- Paris is the city with the highest number of listings and reviews.
- Hotel rooms have the highest average price.
- Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro are among the best-rated cities.
- Hong Kong and Istanbul have comparatively lower overall ratings.
- Cleanliness and value for money are generally the lowest-scoring metrics.
